SYS: PURE HARDWARE // NO FIRMWARE
RAW HARDWARE ANALOG 555 TIMER

Touch-Activated
555 Audio Synth

A compact, touch-sensitive analog sound toy that generates retro 8-bit audio frequencies using human skin resistance. Zero microcontrollers required.

ASTABLE MULTIVIBRATOR

Why We Built It

To step away from code and experiment with basic analog oscillation. This project serves as a hands-on learning experience for active electronic components, resulting in a playable instrument built entirely without microcontrollers.

Inspiration & Clones

Inspired by and cloned from the Blix Squishy Circuits educational toy (featured on Shark Tank India), combined with the DNA of classic 1970s DIY 555-timer noise boxes like the legendary Atari Punk Console.

The raw hardware required to generate the oscillation.

1x 8 Ω Speaker
1x 9V Battery & Clip
2x Touch Pads (Copper plates or coins)
MODULE 02 // THEORY

How It Works

1

The Heartbeat

The NE555 IC acts as an astable multivibrator (an oscillator), flipping its output ON and OFF rapidly to drive the speaker membrane.

2

Setting the Frequency

The 103 ceramic capacitor constantly charges and discharges. The speed of this cycle determines the baseline oscillation frequency.

3

Human Resistance

The user's skin acts as a variable resistor bridging the two touch pads. Pressing harder or changing the contact area alters electrical resistance, instantly shifting the pitch (Chniii... to Chnuuu...).

4

Audio Filtering

The 100 µF electrolytic capacitor blocks DC power from the battery while letting the AC audio wave pass safely through to the 8 Ω speaker, preventing damage.
